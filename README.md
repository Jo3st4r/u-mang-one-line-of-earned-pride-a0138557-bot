## The line

> At 2 a.m. his agent was still building while he slept, and by sunrise the boy who had never won anything finally had 27 entries in the race.

2 a.m., 27 entries, 1 boy who never won: the scene is a real
night — the agent still building while the boy sleeps — and the
payoff lands at sunrise, when a long overnight effort becomes
entries in a race he had never been in before. It is true, it is
earned, and it is quiet: nobody watched it happen, nobody
applauded, and the pride is in the count, not in the telling.
